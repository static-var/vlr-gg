## Promotional text

Follow the matches that matter to you. Save favorites, hide spoilers, and keep live scores and upcoming matches on your Home Screen.

## Description

Follow competitive Valorant in one place. Val Esports brings together match schedules, live scores, results, events, and regional rankings.

YOUR FAVORITES, TOGETHER
Save teams, players, events, and matches to build a home feed around the competition you follow. No account or sign-in is required.

FOLLOW EVERY MATCH
Check upcoming match times, live series scores, completed results, maps, and player statistics. Add upcoming matches to your calendar.

KEEP RESULTS A SURPRISE
Turn on spoiler protection to hide scores until you are ready to see them.

FOLLOW THE WHOLE EVENT
Explore event schedules, participating teams, standings, and prize information. Check regional rankings.

AT A GLANCE ON iPHONE AND iPAD
Add favorite-match widgets to your Home Screen. Find saved favorites through Spotlight and use supported Shortcuts to check your next favorite match.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose a theme and enjoy a clean layout on iPhone and iPad.

Val Esports is an independent, open-source fan app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games or VLR.gg. Match information is sourced from VLR.gg. Valorant and related marks belong to Riot Games.

## Review notes

No account or sign-in is required. An internet connection is needed to refresh match schedules, results, and rankings.

To test favorites, open a team, player, event, or match and use its favorite control. The Home screen then shows relevant matches and events. To test widgets, first save a match or team, then add a Val Esports widget from the iOS widget gallery. Widget refresh timing is managed by iOS.

Spoiler protection can be toggled in the app. Favorites can be opened through Spotlight after they have been indexed. Supported app actions are also available in the Shortcuts app.

There are no in-app purchases in this version.
