Follow competitive Valorant in one place. Val Esports brings together match schedules, live scores, results, events, and regional rankings.

YOUR FAVORITES, TOGETHER
Save teams, players, events, and matches to build a home feed around the competition you follow. No account or sign-in is required.

FOLLOW EVERY MATCH
Check upcoming match times, live series scores, completed results, maps, and player statistics. Add upcoming matches to your calendar.

KEEP RESULTS A SURPRISE
Turn on spoiler protection to hide scores until you are ready to see them.

FOLLOW THE WHOLE EVENT
Explore event schedules, participating teams, standings, and prize information. Check regional rankings.

AT A GLANCE ON iPHONE AND iPAD
Add favorite-match widgets to your Home Screen. Find saved favorites through Spotlight and use supported Shortcuts to check your next favorite match.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose a theme and enjoy a clean layout on iPhone and iPad.
