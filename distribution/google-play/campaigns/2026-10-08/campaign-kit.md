## Positioning

Brazil: follow VALORANT scores and favorite teams without ads or sign-in. Demonstrate the Android widget and spoiler controls. Use Brazilian Portuguese.

India: follow match schedules, scores and favorites on Android. Use English as the main campaign language, with Hindi as an additional option. Do not assume all Indian fans speak Hindi. Verify the current timezone behavior before making local-time scheduling the main promotion.

Full campaign descriptions are saved in the linked Console drafts. They cover favorites, match details, calendar export, spoiler controls, widgets, events and rankings; retain the former VLR.gg name and independent/unofficial disclosures; and qualify widget background refresh behavior.

## Distribution plan

Use a small organic pilot after the listings are published and their links verified on Android.

1. Share one real match-day demo on the app's own social accounts, using the appropriate language and campaign link.
2. Identify a small set of active VALORANT community moderators and esports creators in each country. Review posting rules and audience relevance before approaching anyone. No recipients or partnerships have been selected.
3. Prepare a short creator/community message with the actual app demo. Ask whether a post is welcome. Consider unpaid coverage only. Do not send messages until the user specifies recipients and authorizes that outreach.
4. Publish the widget or spoiler demo as the next creative. Avoid duplicate promotional posts in the same community.
5. Review campaign visitors and install-click rate weekly once launched. Use completed acquisitions and app engagement where attribution is available. Do not report install clicks as installations.

Suggested own-account channels: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Consider regional Discord communities and relevant Reddit communities only where promotional posts are allowed. X can carry the short demo and match-day post. Account access, recipient selection and publishing are not part of the saved drafts.

## Measurement and decisions

Baseline from Sept 7–Oct 4, 2026: Brazil 159 visitors and 22.01% install-click rate; India 300 visitors and 12.33%. These are country-wide baselines, not campaign results.

Use the listing dimension for campaign-page performance and UTM source/campaign breakdowns for individual channels once tagged links are prepared. Keep campaign link identifiers stable. Record creative, language, destination, publish date, visitors and unique install clicks. Review successful acquisition and returning usage separately where attribution allows it.

The 50% listing rollout is a pilot setting, not a configured Store Listing Experiment. Differences between visitors who receive the campaign page and default page do not by themselves establish a causal improvement.

An initial 14-day distribution pilot is reasonable, but there is no preset statistical winner date. Extend measurement when the sample is small. No promised download target is included. The campaign budget is ₹0.

Launch prerequisites: campaign listings published and reviewed, links checked on Android, current timezone behavior verified, and distribution accounts/communities selected. Every activity must be free. No automatic future monitoring has been scheduled.
