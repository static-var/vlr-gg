# Official Apple device frames

Downloaded from [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/).

- iPhone 18 Pro, Silver, portrait: original PNG 1350 × 2760. Screen rectangle: x=72, y=69, width=1206, height=2622. Source: https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Bezel-iPhone-18.dmg
- iPad Pro 13-inch (M5), Silver, portrait: original PNG 2300 × 3000. Screen rectangle: x=118, y=124, width=2064, height=2752. Source: https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Bezel-iPad-Pro-%28M5%29.dmg

Original frame PNGs are unchanged. The `*-screen-mask.png` files isolate the connected transparent display area from each original PNG and expand it two pixels underneath the opaque bezel to avoid anti-aliasing gaps. They are cropped to the screen rectangles above. The camera overlay remains in the original frame. Native screenshots fit these rectangles without cropping or stretching.
