Prism derives its surfaces and semantic colors from the official palette.
It blends raised surfaces and tinted containers, brightens dark-flavour metadata,
and darkens Latte action and status foregrounds for readable small labels.
Typography remains that of Prism. Catppuccin uses a rounded Material shape scale.
