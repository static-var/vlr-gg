The next match. A favorite team. The tournament everyone is watching. We want keeping up with competitive Valorant to feel simple.

How we got here

Our journey as Valorant Esports took shape in late 2022 and 2023. The idea was to bring matches, scores and the wider competitive scene together in an app that's easy to use.

Since then, the Android app has reached 10,000+ downloads on Google Play. Today, you can follow the game on Android and iOS, with your favorites close by and no app account required.

Explore the source code

Giving users the best experience is our highest priority.

That means making the everyday things easier: finding match schedules, checking results and keeping track of your favorites.

Watching later? No-spoiler mode helps you catch up at your own pace. Native widgets put a quick match check on your home screen.

When something gets in the way, we want to hear about it. We build independently and are fully bootstrapped, with no external funding.
