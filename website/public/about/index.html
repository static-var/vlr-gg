How we got here

Our journey as Valorant Esports took shape in late 2022 and 2023. The idea was to bring matches, scores and the wider competitive scene together in an app that's easy to use.

Since then, the app has reached more than 10,000 installs. Today, you can follow the game on Android and iOS, with your favorites close by and no app account required.

Giving users the best experience is our highest priority.

Watching later? No-spoiler mode helps you catch up at your own pace. Native widgets put a quick match check on your home screen.
